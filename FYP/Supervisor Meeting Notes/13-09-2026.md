### Assessment of the seven gaps from a dataset perspective

|Gap|Dataset Requirement|Current Dataset Readiness|My Assessment|
|---|---|--:|---|
|**G1 – Liquidity-aware execution friction**|Individual-stock OHLCV + turnover; ideally bid/ask, intraday volume or order-book data|**Not established**|🔴 **Critical data risk**|
|**G2 – Volatility-memory risk guardrail**|Long historical ASPI/S&P SL20 daily prices covering normal, COVID and crisis regimes|Relatively obtainable|🟢 **Most data-feasible core gap**|
|**G3 – Sinhala/Singlish financial sentiment**|CSE-related financial text + manually labelled sentiment dataset + existing Sinhala corpus for transfer learning|Dataset must largely be created|🟠 **Feasible but expensive**|
|**G4 – CSE financial-report RAG**|CSE annual/quarterly reports + manually constructed QA/evidence evaluation dataset|Reports likely available; benchmark must be created|🟢/🟠|
|**G5 – Sentiment × return/liquidity relationship**|Timestamped sentiment + ticker mapping + returns + turnover + liquidity measures|Depends heavily on G3 + market history|🟠|
|**G6 – Explainability**|Uses datasets from other components|No independent dataset needed|🟢|
|**G7 – Rigorous evaluation**|Long CSE price/volume history sufficient for walk-forward testing and multiple regimes|**Not established**|🔴 **Critical project gate**|
