### 6.4 Methodology and Development Approach

**65/100, Good. Weight 15%, contributing 9.75.** Priority: **Critical.** Needs a complete experimental protocol, not only methodology labels.

**Working well.** Slide 13 correctly separates Design Science Research from evolutionary prototyping. Meaningful experimental safeguards are identified: chronological evaluation, common cached agent outputs across competing methods, ablations, multiple seeds, regime decomposition, HAC and bootstrap inference, point-in-time access controls. The Research Gap Matrix contains significantly more methodological detail than the slides currently communicate, including persistence-estimation instability, liquidity-proxy limitations, comparability of heterogeneous-agent confidence, and the possibility that the approach may fail to outperform equal weighting. These are signs of genuine critical research design.

**Why not higher.** The methodology is not specified tightly enough to make the planned experiments fully reproducible. There is no complete formal definition of the weighting algorithm, its optimisation objective, constraints or update procedure in the submitted slides.

Three specific technical concerns:

| Concern | Issue |
|---|---|
| **Oracle arm** | An oracle using future information is a diagnostic upper bound, not a deployable strategy against which fairness can be assessed in the same way as other methods |
| **Success condition, slide 7** | Exceeding equal weighting by more than its own interquartile range needs a precise statistical definition. A full-window return is a single observation; the interquartile range must be defined over a meaningful sampling distribution |
| **Execution-cost term** | A nonlinear execution-cost term cannot simply be inserted into a convex allocation objective without proving the resulting optimisation retains the required mathematical properties |

**To reach 100, research methodology:**

- [ ] Define the Design Science Research process and its phases specifically for this project
- [ ] State which research question is answered by each experiment
