Reflection model
Chain of Thought (CoT) prompting
Evaluates visual data (ie Charts)


Contains five primary modules
	1. **Summarize module**
		Large volumes of news data => concise summaries that highlight factual information
	2. **Technical Analyst module**
		Visual reasoning capabilities of LLMs to analyze candlestick charts with technical indicators providing interpretations of next-day trading strategies
	3. **Reflection module**
		1. Assess short term and medium term performance of previous trades
		2. Plot past trading signals, generate charts, and offer insights into the effectiveness of trades
	4. **Prediction module**
		Integrates information from all 3 components to forecast trading actions to determine position size as a percentage of the portfolio, and provide a detailed explanation of the decision.
	5. **Reward module**
		Executes trades and calculates performance metrics based on prediction modules output.
