## Reading the sessions together

> Because the panel member changes week to week, a point raised in one session may not have been heard by the next. Where a later session repeats or contradicts an earlier one, record it in that session's note under *Interaction with Existing Direction* rather than treating the panel as a single consistent voice.

Rooms 2 and 1 have come up so far, and Room 2 twice. Where the same member recurs, the session is continuous with the earlier one and can build on it. Where it does not, assume nothing from previous weeks has been heard.

The data window is the clearest case: deferred in Week 4 by Mr Salitha, answered in Week 5 by Mr Sanjula, at a different scale. Neither of them knows what the other said, so reconciling them is my job and the supervisor's, not theirs.
