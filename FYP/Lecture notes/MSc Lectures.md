## Week 3 (AI Usage in Research)

### Consensus AI

Traditional GenAI tools like Gemini, ChatGPT etc have a problem where if ur looking for research it will provide all the papers that are related to it, without considering the quality of the paper. So very high of chance getting low quality papers just because it supports the research.

To solve this use [Consensus AI](https://consensus.app/)

Ideal filters
	Published Year: Last 5 Yrs
	Journal Rank: Q1 to Q3
	Exclude Preprints

Optional Filters
	Methodolody: Literature Review
	Country Filter
