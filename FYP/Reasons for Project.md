Ablation study - removing core components

The strongest direction is no longer:

> **“Understand why CSE moves.”**

That is far too broad and effectively becomes financial-market research rather than a focused Computer Science FYP.

The stronger Computer Science research is:

> **Design and evaluate an adaptive multi-agent financial decision framework whose orchestration changes according to liquidity, volatility persistence and evidence reliability, using the CSE as the frontier-market case study.**

Then the study can additionally answer an important empirical question:

> **When does local sentiment actually contribute useful information in CSE decision-making, and when should the system reduce its influence?**

That is a coherent research story: **one main technological contribution, two defensible sub-gaps, multiple datasets, and controlled experiments capable of proving or disproving the student's assumptions.**
